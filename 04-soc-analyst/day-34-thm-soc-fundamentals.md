## SOC Operating Model

### People

The team members who review, investigate, decide, and respond.

### Process

The repeatable steps used to handle security activity.

### Technology

The systems and tools that provide information and support investigation.

### Combined View

```text
People use Technology
        ↓
They follow a Process
        ↓
The SOC performs defensive security work
```

## Practical Relevance

A functioning SOC depends on:

- A clear purpose
- Defined components
- Trained people
- Repeatable processes
- Supporting technology
- Practical application

## Key Takeaways

- A SOC is a team-based defensive-security function.
- SOC work depends on people, process, and technology.
- Technology alone is not enough.
- Repeatable processes help teams handle security activity consistently.
- Practical exercises connect SOC concepts to operational thinking.
