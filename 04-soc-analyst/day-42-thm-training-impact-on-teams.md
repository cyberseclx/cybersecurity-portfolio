## Objective

Understand how structured cyber security training improves individual capability, team performance, organisational readiness, and skill development.

## Task 1 – Understanding the Impact of Cyber Security Training

Cyber security skills are built through practice and training.

The room emphasised that nobody starts as a cyber security expert. Skills must be developed continuously and kept current.

Because cyber security practice can affect systems and networks, learning should take place in safe training environments rather than on production systems.

## Why Training Matters

Training helps teams prepare before a real incident happens.

The room explained that it is better to learn new concepts, techniques, and tools in a controlled environment instead of trying to learn them during a live cyber attack.

Proper training can improve the quality of the cyber security team's work and reduce the likelihood of incidents.

## Organisational Impact

A major organisational benefit of training is increased team capacity without immediately hiring more people.

The reason is that trained employees are better prepared to handle situations when they occur.

Instead of learning during an attack, the team already has experience with relevant concepts, techniques, and tools.

## Junior Hiring and Skill Ramp-Up

Training makes it more practical to hire junior staff.

It helps junior employees:

- Build skills faster.
- Develop practical confidence.
- Understand tools and techniques before handling real incidents.
- Become productive more quickly.

It also reduces the amount of repeated basic training senior employees need to provide.

## Centralised Training

Centralised training can create a common baseline for skills and knowledge across an organisation.

This can help organisations:

- Assess employee capability more clearly.
- Track skill development.
- Assign tasks based on actual skills.
- Identify gaps.
- Define clearer development paths.

Instead of relying only on broad labels such as "junior" or "senior," organisations can evaluate specific skills.

## Teamwork and Training

Training can also improve teamwork.

The room used CTF-style activities as an example of how team-based cyber security exercises can help people practise together and build stronger working relationships.
