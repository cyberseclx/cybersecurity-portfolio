## Task 1 - Introduction to Digital Forensics

Forensics is the application of methods and procedures to investigate and solve crimes.

The branch of forensics that investigates cyber crimes is called **digital forensics**.

A cyber crime is criminal activity conducted on or using a digital device.

Digital forensics uses tools and techniques to investigate digital devices, identify evidence, analyze it, and support necessary legal action.

---

## Example Investigation Scenario

Law enforcement searched a bank robber's residence using proper search warrants.

The following digital devices were found:

- Laptop
- Mobile phone
- Hard drive
- USB device

The case was handed to the **digital forensics team**.

The team securely collected the evidence and investigated it inside a digital forensics laboratory equipped with forensic tools.

## Evidence Found

### Laptop

- A digital map of the bank used for planning the robbery

### Hard Drive

- A document listing the bank's physical security controls
- Plans created to bypass those security measures

### Mobile Phone

The mobile phone contained:

- Illegal chat groups
- Call records connected to the bank robbery
